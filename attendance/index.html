What Indiana asks for

  • Indiana treats a home school as a non-accredited private school. Parents must keep attendance records for each student.
  • Instruction must be for the same number of days as public schools: 180 days a year. There is no hourly or subject-hour requirement.
  • Records do not get filed anywhere. Keep them on hand in case the local superintendent or the Department of Education asks to see them.
  • Download the CSV (or print the log) at the end of each school year and keep a copy with your records.
