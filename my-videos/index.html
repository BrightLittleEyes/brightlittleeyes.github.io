    Choosing without touch

    The same choosing system as I Can Learn To See. Look and hold: she looks at a choice until it fills with red. Look and press: she looks, then presses a switch (Space or Enter). Pointer works with Mill Mouse, a Tobii tracker, Windows Eye Control or a head-mouse. If Mill Mouse also clicks, that's fine: one look still only chooses once.
