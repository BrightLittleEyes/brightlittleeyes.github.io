Head sensitivity

Touch: tap to choose. Look and hold: rest the pointer on a choice; it fills with red, then it is chosen. Look and press: point, then press a switch (Space or Enter) or click. Pointer works with Windows Eye Control, Tobii gaze-to-cursor, Mill Mouse or a head-mouse. Camera is a webcam head pointer (Windows). Grown-ups: press and hold the gear to open Settings.
