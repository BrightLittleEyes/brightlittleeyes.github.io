My Badges

For grown-ups

IREAD-3 has a few questions where kids read a short passage with two blanks and choose the word that fits each blank from four choices. Indiana's standard for this is 3.RC.11, using context clues to figure out unknown words. IREAD Practice uses that same format. Word Detective teaches the skill underneath: Ruth picks the meaning, then taps the clue word that proves it. Tilly, her robot ballerina, cheers her on.

Progress and stars are saved in this browser only. 🔊 buttons read the text aloud. That's good for practice, but on the test she'll read on her own, so try having her read first.

Read with Tilly uses the device's speech-to-text to listen. It's a helper, not a perfect judge. Speech-to-text tends to "fix" small mistakes and sometimes mishears names. After she reads, you can tap any word to switch it between right and missed.

The 4 Detective Steps

Use these every time you see a word you don't know.

1

🛑 Stop

Stop at the tricky word. Don't skip it!

2

👀 Look

Read the words before and after it. Hunt for clues.

3

🔄 Swap

Try putting each answer in place of the word.

4

✅ Check

Does the sentence still make sense? Then you've cracked it!

The 5 Kinds of Clues

IREAD Test Tips 💡

  • Read the whole passage first, before you pick anything.
  • For each blank, reread the sentence with the blank and the sentence after it. Clues often come next!
  • Try every answer choice in the blank. Read it in your head.
  • Cross out choices that sound silly. Pick the one that makes the most sense.
  • Some words have more than one meaning, like bark or pitch. The clues tell you which meaning fits.
